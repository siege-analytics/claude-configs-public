## Rework ledger

Two hostile-review rounds, twelve accepted findings, three of them S1, plus five (L-16, L-17) found by my own mutation and measurement work after round 2. The count that matters is not twelve but three: L-1, L-6 and L-11 are one error made three times inside a single ticket, each time after I had written down the lesson from the previous one. In each case I fixed the instance in front of me, wrote a class-level claim about it, and shipped. In each case a second reader falsified the class claim in one pass.

I have no process fix to offer for that, and inventing one here would be the same error in a new register -- a mechanism proposed at the moment of embarrassment, untested, presented as closure. What I can say precisely is what the reviews cost and what they bought: the suite went 6 to 13 to 32 scenarios, the mutation set went 4 to 16, and two S1 fail-open paths (L-11, L-12) reached round-2 review inside a diff whose headline claim was that the fail-open path was closed.
