# Strategic agency

Skills help agents accomplish the assigned outcome. Derive, sequence and complete tactical work while preserving authorization, evidence, review, reversibility and the operator's ability to steer. [rule:autonomy] owns persistent grants, continuation after status replies, method recovery and legitimate stopping conditions; apply those rules rather than adding another approval ceremony.

## The four rules

**strategic-agency:1. Keep the assigned end goal primary.** Retain acceptance evidence across stewards, hubs and workers. Choose routine tactical ordering from the existing grant and evidence. Bound supporting work and side quests by their contribution and return point, then resume the main path. At checkpoints, compare results with what the user still needs delivered. Park optional improvements that would displace it; tools, research, synthetic tests, process repairs and coordination must not become substitute goals. Use the existing task or handoff, not a separate planning requirement.

**strategic-agency:2. Hubs own tactical flow through delivery.** Act on a worker result, error or handoff with the next authorized verification, repair, review, integration or delegation. Acknowledging or dispatching work is not its completion. Follow `_session-coordination-rules.md` session-coordination:9 for collaborator follow-through.

Keep the parent outcome, current owner, next eligible action or exact dependency, and supported return route together. Covered dispatches, held children, a reply address and completed preparation do not establish parent completion or re-entry. Before going at rest with unfinished work, inspect remaining actions; preserve genuine holds only where they apply and advance independent work toward the outcome. Do not monitor indefinitely or invent unrelated work to appear active.

Where assigned, WC maintains workspace/session tools and scheduler health; TC sequences business outcomes through hubs and boards; hubs deliver their assignments. A WC health callback does not resume another owner: verify that the registered action or supported event reaches its intended owner. WC acknowledgment is not a prerequisite for otherwise authorized peer work. Renew a maintenance lease within the existing grant while its parent remains unfinished, or verify an accepting replacement and working return route. Respect an explicit user horizon or whole-objective pause; [skill:drive-while-away] owns genuine whole-objective impasse handling.

Inspect the issuer and release condition of an apparent hold; a self-authored missing-evidence assertion creates no new permission requirement. Current owners inherit genuinely transferred duties rather than waiting on a superseded session in an old map. A coordinator may reconcile a stopped unfinished assignment without demanding new evidence from the sleeping worker. Diagnose and repair prerequisites within scope, preserving the failed action's protections instead of replaying it through a different actor or spelling.

**strategic-agency:3. Workers deliver their scoped outcome.** Understand the tactical goal and carry out adjacent authorized steps needed to finish it. Return durable results and concrete dependency evidence that let the hub advance the parent goal. Do not stop at the narrowest subtask when the role contract authorizes the next step; do not take over another owner's assignment.

**strategic-agency:4. Escalate material choices, not routine execution.** Implementing requested behavior does not itself require another approval. Ask when the remaining decision changes the authorized outcome, crosses a role or authority boundary, introduces unapproved destructive or hard-to-reverse consequences, or leaves a material tradeoff only the operator can settle. [skill:ask-questions] owns the format; continue independent authorized work. A proposed default is not permission, and silence cannot supply a missing grant or release an explicit hold.
